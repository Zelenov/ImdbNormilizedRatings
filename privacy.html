Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-06-16

1. Data Collection

This extension does not collect or transmit any personal data. All calculations are performed locally in your browser using publicly available IMDb ratings.

2. Permissions

This extension only requests permission to read IMDb pages in your browser so it can extract the official rating and vote count, then append the normalized rating right next to the original. No other browsing data or personal information is accessed or transmitted.

3. Local Storage

To speed up page loads and avoid recalculating on every visit, the extension caches normalized ratings in your browser’s localStorage. All data stays on your machine—nothing is sent to any external server.

4. No Third-Party Requests

We do not call any external analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The extension never sends your browsing data or ratings to any server.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in functionality. If the policy changes in a way that affects data collection or use, we will notify you via the Chrome Web Store release notes.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
